Privacy Policy

{{ app_name }} · last updated 2026-11-16

1. Data we process

  • Videos you provide (uploads or links) and the files made from them (audio, transcript, translation, dubbed video, captions). They are stored on the server only to produce your result and are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Speech-to-text runs on the server itself. The transcript text is sent to Google's Gemini API for translation, and the Burmese text to a text-to-speech service to create the voice.
  • Feedback you choose to send (rating, message) together with your IP address and time.
  • Basic logs (IP address, time) for rate limiting and abuse protection.

2. TikTok data

If the site owner connects a TikTok account, we receive from TikTok, with permission: the account's open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic), and the right to upload videos to that account (video.upload, video.publish). The access and refresh tokens are stored only on this server, are used only to post videos the user explicitly chooses to post, and are deleted when the account is disconnected. We do not read other TikTok content, and we do not sell or share TikTok data with anyone.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the services needed to do the work described above (translation, text-to-speech, and TikTok when you post).

4. Your choices

  • Do not upload videos containing personal data you do not want processed.
  • Disconnect TikTok at any time on the site or in the TikTok app.
